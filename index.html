How to Play 🎨

Easy, satisfying, and relaxing rules:

1️⃣
Pick a Color Number
Tap any numbered swatch from the bottom palette tray.
2️⃣
Tap Pulsing Shapes
Matching uncolored shapes will gently pulse. Tap them to fill!
🔥
Build Combo Streaks
Fill shapes in rapid succession to unlock ascending melodic chimes!
⭐
Collect 3 Stars
Complete puzzles without hints to achieve a pristine 3-star rating!
⌨️
Desktop & Keyboard Controls
Keys 1–9 select colors, H for hint, Z for zoom, Space/P to pause, Esc to back. Scroll wheel zooms in/out!
